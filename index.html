clipper

clipper is a private tool used by the owner of this site to turn long videos that the owner is authorized to use (for example, under clipping campaigns that explicitly allow it) into short vertical clips with subtitles.

Every clip is reviewed and approved by the owner before publication. Approved clips are uploaded only to the owner's own social media accounts (one account per platform) through each platform's official API, with daily volume limits.

clipper is not offered to other users and does not access any data from other people's accounts.

▶ YouTube

clipper uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's approved clips to the owner's own YouTube channel. By using clipper you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy.
